# Tuku — original playful runner

**Approved archetype:** original, friendly, mischievous young-adult trickster in contemporary Kathmandu; not a direct Jhyalincha portrayal or ritual performer.  
**Approved clothing approach:** modern everyday outfit with restrained Newar textile-inspired craftsmanship details, no sacred/ceremonial copy.  
**Still proposed:** working name Tuku, exact facial design, precise colors/outfit geometry, gender presentation, all model details and final visual sign-off.

## Personality and gameplay silhouette

Quick-witted, a bit overconfident, warm-hearted, playful and agile. Readability **from behind on a mobile screen** is more important than detailed face work. Avoid fantasy armor, villainy or exaggerated anime styling.

- **Front:** expressive brows, short scarf knot on wearer's left, open jacket, diagonal strap.
- **Left:** clear jacket/shirt hem separation; bag mostly far side.
- **Right:** satchel clearance from elbow and swinging thigh; anchored strap.
- **Back:** largest shape is teal jacket, diagonal strap, cream shirt hem and distinct slim charcoal legs; main gameplay view.
- Optional front three-quarter personality view, rear three-quarter jogging pose and detail insets.
- Preview full back silhouette at **~120 px** and **~70 px** tall.

## Cultural design limits

Use **original** small band/stripe/weave detailing on scarf/cuff, inspired by craftsmanship documented in Newar dress. Do **not** copy hāku patāsi, tapālan literally, Jhyalincha attire, ritual masks, sacred marks, ceremonial props or unverified exact patterns. An original texture must not be advertised as a traditional motif without review.

## Codex's asset sequence, only after approval

1. Create/validate the concept turnaround sheet (art review gate).
2. Blender low-detail **static silhouette blockout** from approved sheet; provide `.blend`, reproducible script if procedural, `.glb`, neutral 4-view renders and gameplay rear-camera screenshot.
3. Review on phone at real gameplay framing.
4. **Separate future ticket** for full rig, RUN, JUMP and end-of-run animations. Avoid cloth physics and a complex face rig.

Illustrative future triangle/material targets may be 5k–10k triangles and 1–2 materials; **performance tests determine actual budget**, not these guessed numbers.
