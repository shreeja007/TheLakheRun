## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This review pack establishes the creative boundaries, cultural provenance, and visual design proposal for the guardian character **Lakhey** in *Lakhey Run: The Guardian's Challenge*.

The objective is to ensure that the character is portrayed respectfully as a **benevolent, energetic, and commanding festival spirit guardian** in a joyful, nonviolent challenge, while strictly avoiding horror tropes, demonization, or inaccurate claims of authentic ritual reenactment.

## 3. Visual & Technical Specification (Proposal v0.1)

### Proportions & Silhouette Contrast
- **Height:** 2.35m (1.4× scale relative to Tuku's 1.68m frame).
- **Shoulder / Chest Span:** 0.85m broad, imposing athletic build.
- **Silhouette Dynamics:** Broad upper torso tapering to a flared, swirling pleated skirt that fans out during sprints and turns.
- **Mane:** Voluminous, cascading dark mane (`#1A1412` with deep crimson undertones `#7F1D1D`) framing the head and cascading over shoulders.

### Palette & Materials
- **Primary Vestment:** Deep Ceremonial Crimson (`#A31D1D`, matte canvas/silk feel).
- **Ornamental Trim:** Brocade Gold (`#E5A93C`, subtle metallic sheen) on collar yoke, belt, skirt border, and cuffs.
- **Visage:** Warm Terracotta Red (`#C2410C`) with Golden Ochre crest and ivory fangs.
- **Eyes:** Watchful, energetic almond eyes with warm amber glow.

## 4. Cultural Safety Checklist for Stakeholder Review

Before finalizing the 3D blockout (T010) or publishing public promotional assets, the Game Director and consulted cultural reviewers should review the following criteria:

- [ ] **Guardian Framing:** Is Lakhey framed as a respected, spirited guardian rather than a demonic villain or horror trope?
- [ ] **Nonviolent Resolution:** Is the catch mechanic explicitly nonviolent, celebrating high scores with celebratory feedback?
- [ ] **No Sacred Iconography:** Are all mask details and garment patterns original, free from consecrated religious symbols or ritual marks?
- [ ] **Character Independence:** Is Tuku clearly distinct as a modern Kathmandu local, with zero Jhyalincha costume imitation?
- [ ] **Respectful Sound Direction:** Are musical cues (Dhime, Bhusya) originally synthesized and respectful of Newar percussion craft rather than unauthorized ritual recordings?
- [ ] **Documentation Honesty:** Does game documentation explicitly state that this is an original fictional video game, not an authentic festival reenactment?
