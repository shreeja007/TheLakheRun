## Important interpretation

Director agreeing to **explore a character direction** does not automatically approve production-ready costume geometry or culturally sensitive mask art. A Codex ticket may explore a draft only within its explicitly authorized scope. Keep the final art, engine, camera, and gameplay thresholds pending until separately approved.
