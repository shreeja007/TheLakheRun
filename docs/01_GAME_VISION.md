# Game vision and experience

**Owner-approved core:** small, stylized 3D browser game, Kathmandu festival atmosphere, replayable high scores, playful nonviolent guardian pursuit, optional future folklore.

## Player promise

> Enter a colorful Kathmandu-inspired festival street, follow the drums, dodge with quick reactions, and try to stay ahead of the guardian for one more run.

The game should feel **immediately playable**, joyful, spirited, slightly tense and memorable; culturally distinctive because of its *entire environment and sound*, not superficial decorations.

## Emotional arc — proposed

1. **Welcome / wonder:** a few seconds of warm street color and drums.
2. **Playful dare:** a character exchange establishes an agile trickster versus powerful guardian.
3. **Learning:** early obstacles are readable, mistakes have clear feedback.
4. **Momentum:** speed and music intensify without unfair patterns.
5. **Resolution:** a nonviolent catch, visible score, instant retry.

Working experience targets, **not approved numeric requirements**: controls understood within ~5 seconds; runs initially ~1–3 minutes; skippable 8–12 second opening if one proves useful.

## Audience and accessibility

- Casual web players using keyboard or touch, often playing in short breaks.
- Visitors may not know Indra Jatra; the gameplay must not depend on cultural knowledge.
- Keep menus minimal and use readable contrast and clear visual feedback. Sound enhances play but does not carry essential gameplay information alone.

## Design pillars

1. **Fun first:** the smallest satisfying loop beats feature count.
2. **Distinct place and sound:** Kathmandu urban craft, brick, wood, festival rhythm.
3. **Respect living traditions:** distinguish reviewed cultural context from original fiction.
4. **Quick session / quick retry:** no login, tutorials with walls of text, or long reset sequences.
5. **Runs on actual phones:** browser performance determines visual complexity.

## Definition of success

People understand movement, enjoy a short run, notice the distinctive festival atmosphere, understand why they lost, and want to retry. A single enjoyable stage qualifies; multiple half-finished modes do not.

## Future vision (NOT Version 1 commitments)

Short sourced festival-story cards, Pulu Kisi discovery, Indra/parijat story and Rath Pull rhythm challenge are possible *later*. Each must receive a new design and cultural review before implementation.
