# Lakhey — guardian role and visual approval

**Approved narrative role:** powerful, slightly intimidating, energetic but **non-malicious** guardian in a playful fictional chase.  
**Not approved yet:** exact Lakhey mask, costume, facial proportions, performer movement, audio signatures or full hero asset.

## Feel

Tuku moves lightly and quickly; Lakhey moves with commanding weight and rhythm. The contrast gives the chase energy. Avoid treating Lakhey as a generic horror demon or presenting invented movement as an authentic ritual performance.

## Prototype: generic placeholder first

- Start with a distinct large placeholder shape or silhouette behind the runner.
- Show closeness using spacing, framing and harmless feedback rather than violence.
- Catch animation: peaceful end-of-challenge gesture; exact action remains a story/art proposal.
- Keep chase deterministic and easy to tune; no combat or complex enemy AI.

## Final visual direction (design exploration only)

- Carefully research local Majipa Lakhey representation **with knowledgeable practitioners** before final mask, hair, attire, specific gestures or marketing.
- Distinguish hero from Tuku by larger shape and strong rhythm, not merely dark scary colors.
- If final culturally accurate model is expensive, prioritize one reviewed hero asset rather than a large group of under-researched characters.
- Tuku is fictional, and we must not depict their interaction as a certified reenactment of Lakhey–Jhyalincha tradition.

## Separate approval gates

1. Research board with contextual citations and licensing note.
2. Culturally informed review of concept art and movement direction.
3. Owner-approved front/side/back concept sheet.
4. Blender hero blockout and in-game mobile-view screenshots.
5. Rig/animation, lighting and final QA on actual devices.

Do not combine all five into a single Codex assignment. The next specific ticket, `../tasks/T009_LAKHEY_HERO_CONCEPT.md`, is **blocked on a cultural review contact and director art brief**.
