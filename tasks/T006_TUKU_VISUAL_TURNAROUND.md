## Objective

Produce an **inspectable concept/technical turnaround** for Tuku before spending effort on polished 3D character modeling.

## In scope

- Prepare one consistent sheet with FRONT/LEFT/BACK/RIGHT full-body orthographic views and optional 3/4 mood inset.
- Match teal jacket, cream longline shirt, charcoal pants, compact scarf, diagonal strap from wearer left to right-rear bag, and natural runner silhouette **if the director approves those specific details**.
- Deliver a 120px/70px rear-silhouette readability comparison and show placement of all major clothing components.
- Document unresolved art decisions and distinguish a technical turnaround from a polished commissioned illustration.

## Out of scope

- No final character mesh, rig/animation, alternate skins or sacred visual motifs.
- Do not make a different outfit in each orthographic view.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All four views are same height and same A-pose/ground line.
- [ ] Bag, strap, scarf and shirt hem locations are consistent across every view.
- [ ] Rear silhouette is readable at intended mobile scale.
- [ ] No Jhyalincha/ritual clothing or asserted traditional motifs.

## Verification Codex must report

- Supply original editable concept source (e.g., SVG/Blender/procedural drawing), a combined PNG and closeups.
- If Codex cannot create an artist-quality sheet, state limitations; do not claim an unapproved visual is final.

## Deliverables

- Turnaround sheet, editable sources, 4-view consistency checklist and director approval request.

- A completion report at `reports/T006_RESULT.md` using `reports/REPORT_TEMPLATE.md`.

## Stop and ask

No move to final art or 3D modeling until owner approves the concept. If gender presentation or outfit is unresolved, flag it.
