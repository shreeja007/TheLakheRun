# Pulu Kisi draft — source and review notes

T022, derived from the project's T019 Pulu Kisi concept, original prompt and body-proportion correction. The existing concept is the design input; no online photograph, texture or mesh was imported.

The T019 research register is `art-source/festival_phase1_v01/RESEARCH_AND_HANDOFF.md`. It records [ECS Nepal's Pulu Kisi article](https://ecs.com.np/cover-story/pulu-kisi-the-search-for-the-missing-god), the [Kathmandu Post 2025 festival gallery](https://kathmandupost.com/visual-stories/2025/09/06/kathmandu-marks-indra-jatra-with-chariot-processions-and-rituals) and [Commons reference metadata](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pulu_Kisi_dance.jpg). This ticket reuses that recorded research direction; it does not claim new photographic verification or usage rights. Research photographs remain reference only and are not included in this package.

The concept calls for an elongated cloth-covered frame, near-vertical skirt, sculpted head with long curled trunk, reddish fiber tuft and two separated pairs of performer shoes. This model follows those broad proposals. Pleasant two-eye face, simplified ear fans, stripe colors, seams, trim ribbons, flower beads, exact proportions and shoe forms are project-generated concept/blockout choices, not verified traditional costume identifiers.

No actual ritual paintings, sacred forehead marks, deity figures, weapons or choreography are reproduced or invented. It is a simplified draft interpretation, not an authentic ceremonial replica. Its internal arches suggest support only; they are not construction plans or load-bearing engineering.

Director and informed local review are still required for recognizable silhouette, names, respectful simplification and final details before public portrayal. The existing culture document's restrictions on future enemy/obstacle/score-object use remain unresolved. This handoff supplies a static asset only; it does not approve a gameplay role.
