## What the four drafts mean

- **Pulu Kisi:** elongated cloth shell, multiple performer positions, long sculpted trunk and reddish fiber tuft. Body framework/material direction is research-informed. The pleasant face, stripe trim, seam layout, specific shoes, exact dimensions and proportions are original simplified proposals. The actual mask paintings and cloth motifs are deliberately not reproduced. This is not a faithful costume replica; local review must assess recognizable silhouette and appropriate simplification.
- **Kumari rath:** proposed larger base shell in the shared family. Solid wheels, timber framing, pavilion, drawbeam and garland arrangement take broad construction cues from inspected photos. Canopy shingles, joinery, apex cap, relief patterns and trim are stylized approximations; sacred carvings/eye paintings are omitted.
- **Bhairab rath:** compact square companion draft. Squatter pavilion and heavier-looking braces are a modeling proposal, not a verified traditional identifier.
- **Ganesh rath:** narrower companion draft with plain railing panels. Slender posts and simpler trim are a modeling proposal, not a verified traditional identifier. No elephant-headed occupant or statue is invented to distinguish it.

The two smaller raths' individual exact geometry and identifying ornament have NOT been verified against individually identified front/side/back photos. They currently share almost all of the same silhouette and palette. Names label intended asset slots, not certification of accurate reproductions. Do not sell these as three culturally accurate replicas.

Each sheet is framed independently; apparent equal pixel height is not shared world scale. No real dimensions or ratios are asserted. The generated views are approximate drawings, not calibrated renders of a shared mesh. Wheel count/occlusion, rope routes, braces and rail panels need one-geometry reconciliation during modeling.

## Modeling handoff

- Pulu Kisi: separate cloth shell, head, mane, trunk, garland and performer-foot proxies; frame is suggested, not supplied as a load-bearing engineering design. Movement/choreography remains unbuilt.
- Raths: separate solid wheel meshes/axles, chassis, platform, pavilion, roof, drawbeam, ropes, cloth and garlands. Wheels are wood, not modern tires. No deity figures or occupants are supplied.
- For later Meshy input, generate or extract separately framed single-angle images after concept acceptance; the current sheets are for review, not ideal single-image conversion inputs.
- Editable sources here are exact prompts and research briefs; these are raster concepts, not layered paintings, .blend files, GLBs or production rigs.
- Future modeling deliverables require their own ticket with editable .blend/script, GLB, previews and measured budgets.
